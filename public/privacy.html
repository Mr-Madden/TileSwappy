Privacy Policy

1. Overview

TileSwappy is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data in our puzzle game application. We believe in transparency and your right to know exactly what information we do and don't collect.

The Bottom Line: TileSwappy does not require accounts or login credentials, so we don't collect any personal information directly. We do use Google AdSense to show ads, which means Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure those ads. See Section 4 (Analytics & Advertising) for details and how to opt out.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

TileSwappy does not require accounts or login, and we do not directly collect:

  • Personal information - No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Account credentials - No usernames, passwords, or authentication data
  • Location data - No GPS coordinates or location tracking
  • Contact information - No access to your contacts or address book
  • Biometric data - No fingerprints, face scans, or other biometric information
  • Payment information - No credit cards or financial data (the game is completely free)

Our advertising partner independently collects some device and cookie data to serve ads -- see Section 4 below, which covers exactly what that involves.

3. Local Data Storage

Your game data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage technology. This includes:

  • Puzzle completion status - Which puzzles you've solved
  • Solve times - How long it took you to complete each puzzle
  • Current streak - Your consecutive days of puzzle solving
  • Longest streak - Your best streak achievement
  • Game statistics - Total puzzles completed, average times, etc.
  • Preference settings - Any game settings you've configured
Important: This data remains on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser data or switching devices will erase your progress, as there is no cloud backup or account system.

4. Analytics & Advertising

We may use basic web analytics to understand general usage patterns such as:

  • Total number of page views
  • Traffic sources (where visitors come from)
  • General geographic region (country/state level only)
  • Browser and device types

This data is anonymous and aggregated - it does not identify individual users and cannot be linked back to you.

Advertising: TileSwappy shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies (including device and advertising identifiers) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. We do not control what Google collects for this purpose or how long it's retained -- that's governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices: You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ad Settings, or opt out of personalized ads from participating third parties generally at aboutads.info/choices. Most browsers and mobile operating systems also let you limit ad tracking directly in their privacy settings.

5. Cookies

TileSwappy uses cookies and browser storage from two sources:

  • localStorage - To save your game progress (essential for the game to function; set by us, never sent to a server)
  • Advertising cookies - Set by Google AdSense and its partners to serve and measure ads (see Section 4 above)

You can control cookies in your browser settings, but disabling localStorage will prevent the game from saving your progress, and blocking ad cookies may result in less relevant (though not fewer) ads.

6. Children's Privacy

TileSwappy does not knowingly collect any personal information directly from users of any age, and the game itself is suitable for all ages. However, because the site serves ads through Google AdSense, please note:

  • We request that Google serve only non-personalized ads, and/or tag ad requests for child-directed treatment, on pages primarily directed at children, in line with COPPA and Google's own publisher policies.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has been served a personalized ad on this site, please contact us using the details below so we can investigate.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not have actual knowledge that any personal information we might otherwise handle belongs to a child under 13.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no centralized database of user information that could be compromised in a data breach. Your local game data is only as secure as your device and browser.

We recommend:

  • Using a secure device with up-to-date software
  • Being cautious about clearing browser data if you want to preserve progress
  • Using trusted browsers and keeping them updated

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because we don't collect personal data directly, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) largely don't apply to us. However, you have complete control:

  • Local data - You can clear your game progress at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage
  • Cookies - You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings
  • Advertising - See Section 4 for how to opt out of personalized ads

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): California law gives you the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" (a term that includes data shared with ad networks like Google AdSense for personalized advertising), and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we don't collect or store personal information on our own servers, most of these rights are automatically satisfied -- there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. To opt out of the "sharing" that happens through AdSense specifically, use the Google Ad Settings link in Section 4, or set the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser, which we honor as a valid opt-out request. You can also email support@tileswappy.com with any CCPA request.

EU/UK residents (GDPR): Where GDPR applies, our legal basis for the limited processing described in Section 4 (Analytics & Advertising) is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the opt-out links above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. International Users

TileSwappy is accessible worldwide. All game data is stored locally on your device, so there's no server-side international transfer of that data. However, Google AdSense operates globally, and ad-related data it collects (see Section 4) may be processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates, under Google's own data transfer safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated through the app or website.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

11. Legal Compliance

TileSwappy aims to comply with applicable privacy laws including:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - EU
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California, USA
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - USA

We don't collect personal information directly, which limits our own compliance burden considerably -- but because we serve third-party ads through Google AdSense, our advertising partner's data practices (see Section 4) are also part of the overall picture. Google maintains its own compliance programs for these regulations as a certified ad partner.
